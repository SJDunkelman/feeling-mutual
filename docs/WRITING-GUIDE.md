# Writing and publishing on feelingmutual.com

Everything happens in one place, and nothing you do here can break the website.

## Signing in

Go to **www.feelingmutual.com/admin**

Sign in with your email address and password, or with the Google button if you
prefer. That's it — there's nothing to install and nothing to set up.

Bookmark that address. It's the only one you need.

## Writing a new article

1. Click **Articles** in the left-hand menu.
2. Click **New Article** in the top right.
3. Fill in the boxes:

   - **Title** — the headline.
   - **Date** — pick from the calendar. Today's date is filled in already.
   - **Short summary** — one or two sentences. This is the preview text people
     see on the blog page, and what shows up underneath the link in Google.
   - **Main image** — the picture at the top of the article. Drag a file
     straight in, or click to browse. Any size is fine; the website shrinks it
     automatically.
   - **Section** — leave this as *Article* unless it's specifically a client
     case study or a training piece.
   - **Tags** — optional. Type a word, press Enter, repeat.
   - **Article** — the writing itself.

4. Click **Publish** in the top right.

Give it a minute or two, then refresh the website. Your article will be there.

## Writing the article itself

The big box at the bottom works like Word. Select some text and use the toolbar
to make it **bold**, *italic*, a heading, a bulleted list, a quote, or a link.

To put a picture in the middle of an article, click the **+** button and choose
Image.

You don't need to know any codes or special formatting. If it looks right in
the box, it will look right on the website.

## Saving without publishing

If you want to stop halfway through, click the arrow next to the Publish button
and choose **Save draft**. It'll be waiting for you next time you sign in, and
nothing appears on the website until you actually hit Publish.

## Changing something you've already published

Click **Articles**, click the one you want, make your changes, hit **Publish**
again. The old version is replaced.

## Adding a case study

Same as an article, but click **Case Studies** in the left-hand menu instead.
These are the ones that appear on the home page. There are two extra boxes:

- **Card title** — the heading on the case study card on the home page.
- **Type of work** — a short label like "Customer Journey" or "Brand Strategy
  Development".

## A few things worth knowing

**It takes a minute or two.** The website rebuilds itself after you publish, so
your article won't appear the instant you click the button. Refresh after a
minute. If it's still not there after five, something needs looking at.

**You can't break anything.** Every version of every article is kept
automatically. If something goes wrong it can always be put back.

**A few of the older articles have slideshows or videos built into them.** Five
of them, and the editor doesn't fully understand that kind of content. If you
open an older article and see an embedded video or slide deck in the middle of
it, read it by all means, but ask before changing that one rather than editing
it yourself. Anything you write from scratch is never affected.

**Photos straight off your phone are fine.** Don't worry about resizing or
compressing anything. Upload the original and the website handles it.
