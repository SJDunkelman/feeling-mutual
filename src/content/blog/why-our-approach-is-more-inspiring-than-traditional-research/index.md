Great research is not just information, it's a catalyst for change. The problem is that too much research simply describes what people say, or displays survey data, without actually inspiring stakeholders to act on it. Moreover, if client stakeholders do not buy into the research process in terms of its validity or usefulness, it will not inspire change.

This is why Feeling Mutual goes further to make research deliverables more impactful within client organisations.

## Workshops with senior client stakeholders

We design and facilitate client workshops at both briefing and debriefing stage. This increases their buy-in to the process, helps align disparate client-side silos and means the insights lead to action.

## Multi-media reporting

We generate rich video and photo artefacts from research, which brings insights to life and maximises the impact of the work.

## Robust research methods

We carefully design studies to minimise research effects and maximise the validity of the data we get back.
